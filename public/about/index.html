About us

Aldershot School's FIRST Robotics Competition team, competing in the Ontario district since 2023.

Who we are

We're FRC Team 9263, the ALD Lions, from Aldershot School in Burlington, Ontario. We're a student team: we design, build, wire and program a full-size competition robot each season, with help from our sponsors.

What FRC is

The FIRST Robotics Competition is an international high-school robotics program. Each January a new game is announced. Teams get a few weeks to build a robot for it, then play matches in three-team alliances at events. Ontario runs as a district: teams earn points at district events, and the top teams move on to the FIRST Ontario Provincial Championship.

How an FRC season works

  1. Early January

    Kickoff

    FIRST reveals the new game. Every team in the world sees it at the same moment and starts planning a robot from scratch.

  2. January – February

    Build season

    We design, build, wire and program the robot, then test and fix it until it can play the game reliably.

  3. March – early April

    District events

    Teams play matches in three-team alliances at Ontario district events and earn district points for how they do.

  4. Mid-April

    Provincial Championship

    The top Ontario teams by district points meet at the FIRST Ontario Provincial Championship.

  5. Late April

    FIRST Championship

    The best teams from around the world compete at the FIRST Championship.

  6. May – December

    Off-season

    New members learn the basics, we practise at off-season events, and we raise money for the next season.

Our story so far

  • 2023 — Rookie season. We won the Rookie All Star Award and Highest Rookie Seed at the Western University Engineering Event, and the Rookie Inspiration Award at the FIRST Ontario Provincial Championship.
  • 2025 — We won the University of Waterloo District Event, our first event win.
  • 2026 — We qualified for the Provincial Championship again.
