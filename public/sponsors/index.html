Our sponsors

These businesses and organizations make our 2026 season possible.

Sponsor Team 9263

We're a student robotics team from Aldershot School in Burlington. Every season we design, build and program a full-size competition robot in a few weeks, then take it to events across Ontario. Sponsors make that possible.

Where your support goes

  • Event registration fees
  • Robot parts, motors and electronics
  • Tools and materials for our build space
  • Travel to events
  • Outreach to schools that don't have a robotics program yet

Not just money: parts, machining, tools, software and mentoring help just as much. Ask us what we need this season.
