Our seasons

  1. 2026

    REBUILT season. Competed at the Waterloo and Niagara College district events and qualified for the FIRST Ontario Provincial Championship.

  2. 2025

    REEFSCAPE season. Won the University of Waterloo District Event, our first event win and first blue banner.

  3. 2024

    CRESCENDO season, our second year in the Ontario district.

  4. 2023

    Rookie year. Won the Rookie All Star Award and Highest Rookie Seed at the Western University Engineering Event, then the Rookie Inspiration Award at the FIRST Ontario Provincial Championship.
