## Who we are

We're **FRC Team 9263, the ALD Lions**, the robotics team at Aldershot School
in Burlington, Ontario. We started in 2023 and compete in the FIRST Ontario district.

- **2023**: Rookie All Star Award and Highest Rookie Seed at the Western University
  Engineering Event, then the Rookie Inspiration Award at the FIRST Ontario
  Provincial Championship.
- **2025**: Won the University of Waterloo District Event, our first event win.
- **2026**: Qualified for the FIRST Ontario Provincial Championship.

Full results are on [The Blue Alliance](https://www.thebluealliance.com/team/9263)
and [FRC Events](https://frc-events.firstinspires.org/team/9263).

## What students do

Each January, *FIRST* reveals a new game. Teams have a few weeks to design and
build a robot to play it, then compete in alliances at events across the province.

Students do the real work: mechanical design, building, wiring and programming,
with mentors guiding them. A team also needs people for planning, budgeting,
design, safety and outreach, so there's a place for every kind of student.

## Where your support goes

A robotics season costs real money. Sponsor support pays for things like:

- Robot parts, motors, electronics and raw materials
- Tools and shop equipment
- Event registration fees
- Travel to events
- Team shirts and safety gear
