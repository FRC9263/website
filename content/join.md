## Aldershot students

You don't need any experience. The team needs builders, programmers,
designers, writers, photographers and people who like organizing things.
Email us and we'll tell you when and where we meet.

## Mentors

If you work in engineering, trades, software or business and want to help
students build a robot, we'd love to hear from you.

## Sponsors

Sponsors pay for parts, tools and event fees. See [our sponsors](/sponsors/),
then email us to talk about supporting the 2027 season.
