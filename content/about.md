## Who we are

We're **FRC Team 9263, the ALD Lions**, from Aldershot School in Burlington, Ontario.
We're a student team: we design, build, wire and program a full-size competition
robot each season, with help from our sponsors.

## What FRC is

The *FIRST* Robotics Competition is an international high-school robotics program.
Each January a new game is announced. Teams get a few weeks to build a robot for it,
then play matches in three-team alliances at events. Ontario runs as a district:
teams earn points at district events, and the top teams move on to the
FIRST Ontario Provincial Championship.

## Our story so far

- **2023** — Rookie season. We won the Rookie All Star Award and Highest Rookie
  Seed at the Western University Engineering Event, and the Rookie Inspiration
  Award at the FIRST Ontario Provincial Championship.
- **2025** — We won the University of Waterloo District Event, our first event win.
- **2026** — We qualified for the Provincial Championship again.
